## Euro images (banknotes + coins)

This folder is reserved for **Euro (EUR)** banknote + coin images to be used by the app.

### Important (copyright / licensing)
Realistic images of euro **banknotes** are typically subject to **European Central Bank (ECB)** reproduction rules.
Before adding/bundling any banknote images in this repository, make sure you have the legal right to use them and that
your usage complies with the applicable terms.

If you want a safe approach:
- Use **official ECB-provided assets** (and follow their attribution/usage conditions), or
- Use your **own photos** (taken by you) and ensure your distribution is allowed, or
- Use **illustrations/icons** that do not reproduce banknotes realistically.

### Suggested filename convention (PNG)
If/when we add EUR denominations in code, these names are recommended:

**Banknotes**
- `5eur.png`
- `10eur.png`
- `20eur.png`
- `50eur.png`
- `100eur.png`
- `200eur.png`
- `500eur.png`

**Coins**
- `1cent.png`
- `2cent.png`
- `5cent.png`
- `10cent.png`
- `20cent.png`
- `50cent.png`
- `1eur.png`
- `2eur.png`

### Image guidelines
- PNG with transparent background is preferred.
- Crop tight around the coin/banknote (the app can also trim transparent padding if needed).
- Keep the image upright and centered.
